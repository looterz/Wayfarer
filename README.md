# Wayfarer

An all in one map addon for Classic Era and The Burning Crusade Anniversary.

Wayfarer replaces the stock world map experience with one addon: a properly
sized and behaved map, coordinates, zone levels, fog of war removal, and full
dungeon maps with boss locations.

## Features

- **Screen-sized map.** Size the world map as a percentage of your screen,
  separately for the windowed and maximized states.
- **Fixed map behavior.** Working mouse wheel zoom, an accurate cursor
  position, no screen-dimming backdrop, and a map that stays out of the way
  of the rest of your UI.
- **Move while reading.** The map fades while you move so you can see where
  you are going, with separate opacity for moving and standing still.
- **Coordinates.** Player and cursor coordinates below the map, with
  configurable precision.
- **Zone levels.** The recommended level range and controlling faction shown
  with the zone name under your cursor.
- **Fog of war removal.** See the unexplored parts of every zone, drawn in a
  configurable tint.
- **Dungeon maps.** Full dungeon maps with boss locations and a floor
  switcher, shown automatically when you enter an instance. A dropdown lets
  you browse any dungeon's map from anywhere in the world. Covers every
  dungeon and raid on your client, Classic Era and Burning Crusade alike.
- **Integrations.** Optional Questie quest objectives on the dungeon map,
  AtlasLoot loot tables from boss markers, and click-to-open dungeon entrance
  pins on zone maps.
- **Profiles.** Full settings profiles, shared or per character.

Type `/wayfarer` or `/wayf` to open the settings.

Wayfarer supports WoW Classic Era and The Burning Crusade Anniversary.
Support for Wrath of the Lich King may come later.
